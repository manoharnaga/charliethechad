### Ms. Coconut

A small black scorpion crawls across the hot sand of a lonely jungle beach, searching for food. Its sharp claws move slowly, carefully.

It reaches something — the thing has pores in it and short hairs coming out of the pores, with a few tiny water droplets here and there. The scorpion starts climbing it, soon settling for a nice spot to bite.

> **"HAAA!"**

A young man in his early 20s wakes up, shouting out loud, feeling a strange sensation on his right hand. He keeps shaking his hand violently.

Luckily, the scorpion gets thrown away to a distance.

He stands up and tries to open his eyes, but the bright white light from the sun doesn't allow him to fully open them. He quickly covers his eyes with his left hand.

He can still feel a weird sensation of something crawling on his right hand, and he's feeling the warmth of the hot red sun all over his body — *irritating and hot!*

He starts walking into the jungle for some shade. There are giant Oak trees, Palm trees, Mangroves...

The forest is getting denser and darker as he goes.

Tired, he finds a flat rock, places it near the trunk of a big tree, taking the support of the trunk. He slowly tries to sit on the rock, then leans back onto the trunk to support his spine.

> *"It is more like a chair now."* — He says to himself.

Within a few minutes he becomes fully conscious, out of sleep.

So many thoughts struck his head:

> *"Boat—Ship!"*
>
> *"Alone—People—Friends—Family—Dad—Mom—My Love!"*
>
> *"Survival—Food—Water—Death!"*
>
> *"Snakes—Venom—Wild Animals"*

Fear creeps in thinking about all these things at once. His heart starts pounding with anxiety and hopelessness.

He recalls his father, takes a deep breath to empty his mind.

He picks up a stick with his right hand, clears a small area in front of him with his foot.

> *"Let me note down a few things!"*
>
> *"What is the most important thing that I need now?"*

**Water.** — He writes it down with the stick.

> *"What else do I need to survive in this wild?"*

**Food. Shelter.**

> *"How can I make it out alive from this place?"*

**A boat that could sail for one hundred miles!**

While writing this, the stick breaks and he starts to think back—

> *"Not possible, for real…"* — with grief deep inside.

He stands up—

> **"I am never gonna make it out of here!"**

> *"Always give it another try, son!"* — *"A man never quits!"*

Lines his father used to tell strike his head whenever he thought of quitting something, whether it be football or school.

> *"Ooof…"*

He lets out a long sigh, thinking of the idea that he may have a chance — maybe one in a million — to make it out alive.

As time passes, the hot weather drains all the water in his body.

> **"I AM THIRSTY!"** 
> 
> *"If I continue sitting idle like this, I will probably die of DEHYDRATION!"*
>
> *"NO—No—no"*
>
> *"That is the worst way to die…"*
>
> *"But, how do I get water, in this forest?"* — *"Let me think…"*

> *"Science 101 – We can drink water after boiling it"*
>
> *"Right!"* — *"But I don't have an induction stove here!"*
>
> *"right…"* — his inner voice continuing in a lower tone.

Okay, what else? How else can I get water?

> *"Drink the sea water?"* — *"I will die even faster from dehydration."*

He keeps thinking for some time and something strikes his head.

> **"Transpiration!"** — **"Yes! Transpiration it is!"** 
> 
> *"If I could find the right plants with big leaves that let out water vapor, I would be good."*

Mack starts searching for some plants and trees — the ones with big leaves so that he gets more water.

> *"Yeah! There you go buddy!"*

He finds a tree with branches low enough; he quickly takes a plastic bag from his pocket.

> *"I need to tie this thing tight!"* — *"Oh, there you are"*

He finds a vine plant.

> *"Wait! Let me check something."*

He stomps hard on the ground, sending a warning tremor to drive away snakes and other creatures.

> *"Now we're good"*

He then takes a sharp stone and cuts some vine.

> *"This is some perfect rope to tie!"*

He covers a big bunch of leaves inside the plastic cover and ties it backwards to the same branch with the vine and seals it completely.

> *"I feel like I am killing someone with a plastic cover on the head."* — *"Movies, huh?"* — he says to himself.
>
> *"Okay! My water would be ready in the next three hours…maybe…"*

Mack keeps checking it every five minutes—

> *"Come' on... It has been an hour, and I barely see any water in this!"*

He heads back to the beach to collect anything useful. He walks for a long distance to reach near some rocks where there could be something stuck — something useful that came by the waves of the sea.

He finds two thin plastic water bottles—

> *"Oh, thank God!"*

He washes them both in the sea water and takes them back with him.

While returning, he finds a big broken tent—

> *"So, they left this behind, great…"*

His mind starts to recall his past.

#### Glimpses of the Past

*The ship rose out of the sea like something unreal — all white steel and glass, glowing against the falling sun.*

Mack stepped onto the deck with steady calm, though inside him something surged. Eight months ago, he had been an intern working late nights; now he stood aboard a private corporate vessel as the **youngest Associate Director** in the company.

The air smelled of salt and polished wood. Soft jazz floated through the evening. A steward handed him champagne before he could fully absorb where he was.

> *"Congratulations, sir,"* the man said.

And Mack almost laughed at the word. *Sir.*

As the sky turned molten gold, Chairman Mr. James McConnel called for attention. Conversations quieted instantly. James spoke without theatrics, but with weight. He spoke about discipline under pressure, clarity in chaos, execution without excuses.

Then he turned slightly and gestured toward Mack.

> *"Some people grow into leadership over decades,"* he said. *"Some arrive ready. When he works, he goes into what I call* ***beast mode.***"

The applause that followed was strong and sincere. James lifted his glass toward him.

> *"To the future."*

Mack managed a composed thank you, though his pulse beat hard in his ears. It wasn't ego he felt — it was **validation.** *Earned. Solid. Real.*

Later, when the celebration softened into music and movement, Mack stepped aside to the railing. The ocean stretched endlessly into darkness, the ship slicing through it with quiet power.

He pulled out his phone out of habit. *No signal.*

He stared at the blank screen for a moment and murmured softly:

> *"How are you, Jenny? It's been long since we talked."*

He imagined telling her everything — the speech, the applause, the ridiculous luxury of it all. He slipped the phone back into his pocket and returned to the lights and laughter behind him.

The night felt victorious. The future felt open.

And as the ship moved steadily toward the island somewhere ahead, Mack stood in the glow of success, **unaware that this was the last evening the world would feel this certain.**

### Home Sweet Home

> **"Fire! Fire! FIRE!"**

Mack is again walking on the beach anxiously, holding his stick in the hand.

> *"But how?"* — *"Think Mack! Think!"*

His stomach starts to growl—

> *"I think Mr. Belly needs something really urgent"* — *"I will get back to you in some time, Mr. Fire"*

He walks back to the tent area, breaks open the remaining coconut, drinks all the water, then cuts it into two and eats the coconut flesh.

> *"That was amazing!"* — *"Gotcha!"* — *"I recall something from our school wildlife camp!"* — *"They taught us how to make fire with some wooden sticks"*

Then with pride he says to himself—

> *"Once I get the idea, it is a piece of cake for me."*

He breaks down a big branch of a palm tree, cuts out two or three thin sticks and he also gets a very thick wooden piece from another tree. He also collects a few palm leaves and coconut palm husk fibers (coir).

> *"Fire Drill 101"* — He starts speaking as if he is shooting a video for his social media page, showing all the material.
>
> *"First, we need the fireboard"*

He took a thick wooden piece, but it is not flat, so he starts rubbing it on a big rough rock surface to make it flat.

> *"The fire board is ready!"*

He then picks up two-three sticks, sharpens them, also flattens their surface so that the tiny thorns on the surface won't hurt his palm while holding them.

> *"Now we are ready for the drill!"* — He then senses — *"Something is wrong!"* — *"Our equipment is all wet because of the humid climate"* — *"Okay then, let's dry them up!"*

Mack picks up the board, the sticks, the coconut coir and the palm leaves to the beach side where there is sunlight. He again thinks—

> *"But what if the sea waves reach till here and make them more wet."*

He rolls down a small boulder from a distance and places all the stuff on it. The boulder is placed as such the sea waves won't reach, even if they reach, they're all at a height that the waves can't reach and the sun rays directly fall on the items.

> *"Well done, Mack!"* — Mack thanks himself. *"Sometimes I feel like I am a born genius!"* — He laughs thinking this.

While the fire drill stuff is getting ready, he gets his Transpiration machine ready. He basically again seals a few leaves of a tree with a plastic bag for water.

> *"Now! Let's get some coconuts!"*

He goes for a different tree this time. The job is done in almost two hours; he gets the coconuts back to his tent area.

He completes his meal and returns to the beach to check on the fire drill stuff.

> *"Oh! This is still a bit wet."*

He turns all the items onto the other side.

> *"Completely forgotten it! The tent!"* — *"I planned to finish setting up the tent by tonight."*

He plans for a cuboid-like tent, in which he can fit completely, such that it's sealed from outside during night.

He starts gathering a few poles of wood that would act like four pillars on four corners and some other wooden sticks, poles etc., to support the tent overall.

He puts all the material to the side, then cleans the area with his hands. He then draws a big rectangle box on the ground using a stick — **The foundation marking.**

Now he starts digging holes on the line. He digs with a sharp pole stick of wood and then takes out the loosened soil with his hands and digs again. He digs for almost two feet in depth and two or three inches wide. He then places a pole at a corner, hits it inside using the rock, presses down, then throws in some pebbles and stones for strong support, presses them down with his foot to make it stronger and tighter, then he covers the remaining pit with soil and again stomps it hard enough so that the pole is strong and steady.

He does this for all other poles. After a lot of effort, the skeleton of the camp is ready. He tries to put the tent over the structure. He places it so that the top is covered properly but he observes that the tent is not covering up on the sides, and something can still come inside from the walls.

> *"I need to plan for something!"* — *"Hmmm…."*
>
> *"Oh! The fire board!"*

He quickly runs back to the beach and checks on the fire board material.

> *"Thank God!"* — *"You're all still alive."*

It is almost evening. Before it gets dark, he wants to complete the fire.

Mack places the wooden plate between his legs, holding it tight, then starts to rotate the spindle stick with both hands as fast as he can.

He does this for an hour. His hands start to sweat, and the muscles start to hurt.

> *"Ush! This isn't my piece of cake!"* — he sighs catching his breath.
>
> *"Quick plan!"* — *"I just need to properly cover the walls of my new home sweet home, and I should be good for tonight"*

He carries few logs of dead trees, few branches and poles he breaks them with his legs. He also gets two or three boulders, bunch of rocks, few stones etc.

He also cuts over and gets lots of big palm leaves. They look like banana leaves, not exactly, but they provide good cover.

He then first covers the whole hut with the palm leaves first, then he fixes the poles horizontally across the walls, ties them up with the vine, like the bricks in a wall. At the bottom on each side of the wall, he places boulders and logs for strong support, to not allow any creatures on the ground to enter inside.

> **"Home Sweet Home!"** — he writes down on the right side of the hut.
>
> **"Mack & family live here!"**

It is already night.

> *"Aaaah…"* — he starts yawning — *"Time for bed."*

He has also covered the inside of the tent with dry palm leaves and some wooden logs for warmth and softness.

He lies down inside, places his hands on his chest, slowly closes his eyes and starts thinking—

> *"How are you mom and dad?"* — *"I will come back soon…"*
>
> *"I am waiting to see you, Jenny!"*
